# Basic implementation research

This document summarizes source-derived mechanics for engineering; it is not a replacement manual. Requirements and suggested regressions are in `rules/rule-index.json`. Source verification and implementation coverage are separate statuses.

The authoritative basis is Undying Rules v1.1, accessed from the publisher, plus physical counter and terrain-aid inspection. The game has six playable kingdoms: Fjordland, Oathborn, Eastern Empire, Army of the Night, Goblins and Orcs. Scenarios assign them to Invader and Resistance; players may control more than one kingdom. Alliances, seats, and kingdoms are separate.

Basic play uses whole kingdom turns. Each spring, summer and autumn has one turn per participating kingdom in scenario order. Income Actions precede Income, followed by Activation; winter is skipped. The final season is played before its end-of-season victory check. Kingdom-specific income actions are meaningful choices, especially Night Coven placement and Shashka Laying Waste.

A ready Army moves and then takes one action, becoming finished. Building is interleaved before, between or after activations. Builds inside an own settlement or entry hex are ready; adjacent builds are finished. A settlement made friendly this turn cannot itself host a build, although its adjacent hexes can. Besieged sites block building in and adjacent; recovery is allowed near unbesieged welcoming sites, including allies. Sieges block production rather than movement.

The physical terrain aid confirms Plains and settlement movement cost one; Forest, Mountain and Swamp cost two and add one light defensive die. Road/bridge crossing costs one and can grant the all-road movement bonus. Rivers add one movement point and defense die; major-river hexsides add two of each. Bridges cancel crossing movement penalties while keeping the defense dice. Flying ignores terrain, cannot finish in a prohibited hex, and cannot pass through Lairs.

Light dice are d6 and heavy dice d8; successes require five or more. A result of seven or more is a critical, confirmed by an unmodified light-die success for one additional success. Normal combat applies net successes as hits to the loser. A positive tie favors the only Ranged side, for one hit; zero successes is a draw. Full armies weaken on one hit and are eliminated on another; Fragile armies are eliminated immediately. The inspected weakened counters preserve their printed combat dice and movement.

Stealth permits an optional Ambush if the opponent lacks Stealth. The first Strike ignores terrain, inflicts at most one base hit plus confirmed critical hits, and resolves damage before the survivor's return Strike. Attacking Stealth cannot Ambush a fortified settlement; defending Stealth may do so. City garrisons normally add three light dice even with an Army, while ordinary settlements add one only when unoccupied. Siege Engines cancel one fortification penalty per adjacent allied engine and may attack only unrazed settlements.

Capture is a sequence: loot if eligible, remove enemy control, advance if legal, reevaluate original posture, then place control or raze. Normal loot is two gold, doubled in cities; Shashka gain three, doubled in cities. Huge and unassisted Feral armies do not loot or control. Huge remains adjacent if removal of enemy control reveals a welcoming settlement. Income follows actual control/loyalty changes; razing yields no income. Removing razed markers requires a friendly Army on an underlying welcoming site and two gold.

Oathborn Miners earn one gold by mining; Oathborn and Goblin armies pay one to enter mountains except Siege Engines. Fjordland Rangers enter forests for one and add one light die when attacking, Striking into or defending forests. Fjordland ship movement is free once per activation, with five or eight points, and can be interleaved into normal movement. Feral and Huge units cannot use ships.

Shashka do not collect income. They pay one gold per controlled settlement after optionally laying waste to sites for loot. They collapse on unpaid support or zero control at activation end, except until they have first controlled a settlement. Night Covens add one gold each, add one light die to Night attacks into their site, and support Feral wilderness building and recovery nearby. The Empire rolls for revolts, pays its current revolt level, adds further revolts for any funding deficit, and may suppress one revolt per gold in Activation. Its twentieth revolt causes collapse.

Counter corrections: Akritoi cost one, Ogre movement two. The VASSAL setup slots provide exact Army supply counts and front/back references for all four remaining kingdoms in `sources/unit-provenance-other.json`; the mechanics-only definition file is merged into application content. The optional Vengeful Ghost fortification exception is not a mandatory base rule.
