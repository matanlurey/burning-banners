# Original production artwork

All 27 production raster assets were generated for this project using the built-in image generator and then cropped/compressed locally. No reference-module bitmap is used as production art. Geometry and rule legality are determined exclusively by the typed hex graph and engine.

The visual brief used restrained classic fantasy ink and watercolor: storm-blue skies, red banners, parchment-toned cartography, muted forests, inked mountains and legible troop silhouettes. Five generation briefs supplied the final assets:

1. A wide northern fortress panorama with human, dwarf, orc, goblin and undead armies gathering under red standards.
2. A precise 3×2 grid of six faction portraits, ordered Empire, Fjordland, Oathborn, Orcs, Goblins, Army of Night.
3. A precise 3×2 terrain atlas of clear grassland, forest, mountain, swamp, sea and desert cartographic textures, without labels or hex geometry.
4. A precise 4×2 troop atlas: Freeholders, Sea Reavers, Rangers, Berserkir, Oath Takers, Iron Legion, King's Crossbows and Miners.
5. A transparent 3×2 landmark atlas: village, walled city, coastal keep/docks, dwarf mine, necromancer spire and haunted ruin.

Each atlas was visually checked before grid cropping. Landmark alpha was checked and preserved. WebP production paths, dimensions and SHA-256 values are recorded in `content/assets-manifest.json`. These images are decorative originals, not a certified redraw of any printed map or counter.
