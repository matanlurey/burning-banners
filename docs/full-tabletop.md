# Full tabletop play

Full tabletop is the local, human-adjudicated way to exercise the physical game's entire component set. Its engine handles ordinary legal actions and 181 automated effects. Table controls expose all 148 Magic cards, 38 named Heroes, 44 Army types, 36 Monsters and four Enslaved Hero markers, including the 5 effects that still need printed-rule adjudication. It does not certify missing official campaign setups or enforce every special card rule automatically.

## Set up a printed campaign

1. From Campaign, choose **Create full tabletop · all components**. Choose the boards, years and a campaign name; record the printed source.
2. Begin the campaign. In Council, open **Table**; desktop also has a Table controls rail button. The four tabs are Counters & dice, Private cards, Map & markers, and Campaign ledger.
3. Calibrate terrain, Settlement flags/loyalty/fortification, ports, prohibited/Entry hexes and reciprocal roads/rivers/Sea edges. The templates retain known place inventories but start with blank mechanical assumptions. Their coordinate origins differ from the cropped digital Wildlands map; do not copy hex IDs between packs. Templates separate the boards and do not merge shared half-hexes. Until a join is calibrated, adjudicate the printed crossing with table relocation.
4. Enter the printed opening Armies/Heroes, gold, income, control-marker supplies, alliance assignments, turn order, dates and victory requirements. Starting ledger values are placeholders. Night's five control/Coven markers share one supply; the other printed marker totals remain a source gap.
5. Download or copy a backup after setup. The source and adjudication history travel with it.

Every table mutation requires a rule/reason and validates the resulting state. Counter placement uses finite printed inventories. Card moves preserve one physical copy across hand, owned, discard, removed and deck zones; Hero/Monster pools are finite. Invalid placements and impossible inventories are rejected. A valid state does not establish that the human chose a legal printed timing or fulfilled every special prerequisite.

## Resolve cards, Powers and responses

Choose the correct player seat before viewing Private cards; changing seats opens a handoff curtain. **Play & resolve at table** moves a held Magic card into a pending review and pauses normal actions and AI. Hero Powers can be opened the same way. Use the printed face to choose targets and apply dice/modifiers, costs, recovery, movement, markers or counter changes. Current-activation movement changes require an active unit and update that member's actual remaining budget. A stack can have different movement allowances.

An opposing player may play a response at the table. Reviews nest and must be completed by their owners. Completing the response returns to the preceding review and its seat; completing the original review restores the engine's next actor. Private draws and card moves create owner receipts rather than exposing card identities in opponents' briefings/replays. The pending review, nested responses and random stream survive export/import. An unsellable-only Treasure excess in Winter also pauses AI for an explicit human ruling: no verified printed disposal exception was found. Retaining that excess is logged as a table ruling, not asserted as an official rule.

Counters & dice provides deterministic dice receipts. If a printed effect prevents a pending hit, consume that hit exactly once; any remaining hit still waits for its proper owner. Do not both consume and allocate the same hit. Other controls can relocate a unit/stack, join or separate a Hero, ready/finish/lock a unit, recover/weaken/eliminate it and adjust ledgers. These are human rulings, not substitute automatic interpretations of complex cards.

## Enslaved Heroes

Map & markers holds the separate **four-counter** Enslaved Hero supply, Each marker has Mage and zero printed combat dice; it inherits the accompanying Army’s movement and abilities. Mark an eligible, unaccompanied enemy Army after resolving the printed Enslave instructions. The Army retains its printed kingdom identity and receives a visible Night-control marker; use table controls for its Night-directed actions. Named Hero inventories are not consumed.

Adjudicate the printed Mage rating, mandatory first hit, release placement and failed/locked-caster lifecycle. Remove the marker at the proper time, relocate or eliminate the released Army as required, and apply temporary modifiers separately. These lifecycle choices remain manual; the marker alone does not grant an invented automated Enslave rule.

## Special campaigns and correspondence

Campaign ledger can change alliances, six named turn-order slots, dates, control objectives and campaign labels. For unsupported printed conditions, declare an explicit victory with its reason after the table determines the condition was met. Manual victory mode prevents an unrelated original sandbox deadline from ending the campaign.

The Campaign Desk remains available for return briefings, observable replay, messages/pings, planning notes and delegated AI seats. AI only uses automated actions and pauses for table reviews; another human must resolve unsupported special effects. A closed browser runs no turns. Backups include every seat's private state and require trusted exchange. Hosted async multiplayer is deferred.
