# Source access and authority

Retrieved October 4, 2026. The publisher product page lists the September 23, 2024 Undying Rules and Campaign Notes as current, and says its August 7 corrections are incorporated. No newer publisher rules were located during this pass.

The linked S3 paths contain encoded plus characters and returned 404. Replacing those with encoded spaces retrieved the actual publisher PDFs. The 60-page Undying Rules v1.1 file has SHA-256 `77d904b41730edff5e00b4bba8c61b5dab38dafd5f6e3017ca1140b009e2f3f6`. Its cover says v1.1; the introductory version note still says v1.0. The file was created September 20, 2024 and posted as September 23 material. This internal labeling discrepancy does not make the earlier printed book authoritative.

The BoardGamesBot mirror is byte-identical to this publisher PDF. The mirror was initially useful for discovery, but the final rules authority is the publisher file. Its HTML advertisements, AI assistant, and summaries were excluded.

The August corrections PDF was also retrieved directly. Use it as a component correction ledger and regression checklist. Where its rulebook text differs from the later living book, the September living text governs. For example, the living book allows optional control of a welcoming neutral after conquest; the older correction list used mandatory language. The living Feral rule evaluates hostility after removal of enemy control; the older sheet's description was different.

Physical terrain, combat, and action aids were inspected as component images in the VASSAL v1.7 module. The terrain aid confirms Forest, Mountain and Swamp each cost an additional movement point and add one light defense die. The module also contains individual front and back Army counters and actual duplicate unit slots. Normalized counter mechanics were visually checked; these research images are not included in the public application.

The community Scribd Player Aid is not an operative rule source. It incorrectly allows Flying passage through Lairs, which Undying 8.2 expressly prohibits. It also compresses mine movement in a way that can obscure underlying Mountain terrain. The physical Terrain Effects aid and the living rules resolve both.

Sources that remain incomplete: exact physical control-marker counts for five kingdoms, complete authoritative campaign-book setup beyond the verified introductory material, exact geometry of all four boards, and complete executable card content. These gaps must not be filled with invented official values. The source registry records access, provenance, hashes and distribution status separately.
